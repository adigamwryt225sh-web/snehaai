About This Project

Objective: To provide an accurate, algorithm-driven estimation of placement success based on historical academic data, technical skill levels, and internship experience.


Institution: Built specifically for the students of Chandigarh University to aid in placement preparation, goal setting, and targeting specific company tiers.

How to Use

  • Ensure all academic marks and backlog counts are entered correctly.
  • Your coding skill level heavily influences top-tier company recommendations.
  • Use the sidebar tools to scan your resume keywords, practice technical Q&A, and plan your target CGPA.
  • This tool provides predictive analytics based on general placement trends and is for estimation purposes only.
